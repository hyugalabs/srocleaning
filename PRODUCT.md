# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + lucide-react. Pinned by the requester.

## Users

Owners and facility managers of small-to-mid businesses in Trumbull and Mahoning counties, Ohio (the Mahoning Valley): steel shops, energy providers, medical offices, trucking terminals. They are looking for a dependable local commercial cleaner and want to talk to a person, not a call center.

## Product Purpose

Sarah Ross Office Cleaning, LLC (Girard, OH) is an owner-operated commercial cleaning service. The website should turn a local business owner into a phone call or text asking for a free quote.

Current scope: a speculative hero section, built as a pitch mockup to show the owner how much better her site could look.

## Positioning

A working owner with 25+ years in the valley who cleans the accounts herself. A franchise or regional janitorial company can't honestly say that.

## Operating Context

The primary action is to call or text (330) 880-8254. Email (Sarah.Ross2008@gmail.com) is secondary. Quotes are free.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (from the current site):
- Cyclical cleaning: a plan built around the building's frequency, environment, and traffic.
- Products: she supplies all chemicals and supplies; the client supplies paper products, soap, and dispenser batteries.
- Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) orbital scrubbing and restoration.

## Brand Commitments

- Colors: #323dff blue accent, white ground, black text (from the existing site; pinned by the requester).
- Fonts: Brandon Grotesque Light for headings, Arial for body (pinned). Brandon is a licensed font; the mockup uses the closest obtainable geometric face as a fallback.
- Voice: first-person, warm, plain-spoken, and local ("You work hard - leave the cleaning to us!").

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed: 25+ years in business.
- Also stated on her site: she personally cleans every account, and she's a lifelong Trumbull County resident.
- None exist, so don't fabricate any: testimonials, client logos, review scores, pricing, or certifications.
- Imagery: the mockup uses verified stock photography, labelled as a placeholder for her own photos.

## Product Principles

1. A real person answers. The phone number is the product.
2. Local and long-standing beats slick and corporate.
3. Only claim what she has said herself.
